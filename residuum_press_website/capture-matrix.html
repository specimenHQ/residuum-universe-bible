The Capture Matrix Framework

Domain-General Reference Model v1.0, version date 12 July 2026. Origin case: the Residuum Universe (Wesley F. Black, 2026).

The framework describes capture as a process in which organic signal becomes legible under structural constraint and is converted into a product. Its core formulation is PRODUCT = SIGNAL × CONSTRAINT; in the Residuum-specific model, FREBT = FRET × DEBT.

This is preserved as a framework/reference document, not as a replacement for the current Residuum public canon. Period-specific statements in the July 2026 source are retained as historical provenance.

The source framework includes temporal registers, epistemic layers, capture pathways, residual ambiguity, character archetypes, stylistic guidelines, scale analysis, expansion protocols, and the deeper FREBT matrix.
