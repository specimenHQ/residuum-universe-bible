About the author

Featured author

Wesley F. Black

Wesley F. Black writes restrained literary fiction about technology, bureaucracy, labor, housing, care, and the ordinary human consequences of systems that appear to be working correctly. Residuum Press is an independent press designed to publish additional authors and projects as its catalog grows.
