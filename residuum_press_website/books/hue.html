About HUE

Jonas moves through a shared life with Don from inside a calm, expansive interior world, where ordinary objects, plans, and absences keep accumulating meaning.

HUE follows the ordinary life around memory: mornings, plans, photographs, tickets, texts, rooms, jokes, routines, and the small objects that become an archive almost without anyone deciding that they should.
