## Core thesis

Systems do not conquer; they absorb. Legibility precedes utility, and structural obligation converts organic material into commodity.

## Core equation

**PRODUCT = SIGNAL × CONSTRAINT**

In the Residuum-specific formulation:

**FREBT = FRET × DEBT**

- **FRET / Signal:** organic, human, embodied material such as attention, care, labor, curiosity, grief, memory, desire, and presence.
- **DEBT / Constraint:** structural demands such as quota, policy, credential, record, protocol, access control, safety rule, surveillance, or inherited obligation.
- **FREBT / Product:** the captured output produced when Signal passes through Constraint: categories, records, metrics, identities, credentials, doctrine, infrastructure, algorithms, or culture.
